## 1. What Valor is

A browser-based FPS where clearing an operation pays real money. No download, no
wallet setup, no seed phrase: players sign in with an email and the game creates
a wallet for them behind the scenes. Most of our players had never used a
blockchain before Valor, and most still do not know they are using one.

That is the design goal. The chain is infrastructure, not a feature we make
people learn.

## 4. What we learned that most applicants have not

Valor's Celo economy pays players from a funded pool. We have run that model at
real scale and we have the numbers on why it does not hold:

> **1,154,136 G$ paid out. 73,900 G$ recovered through the shop.**
> **15.6 to 1 outbound.**

The cause was not fraud or abuse. It was arithmetic: one match pays 1,000 G$ and
our most expensive item costs 1,800, so two matches buy everything in the game
and the shop stops being a sink. That is the same failure that killed an
estimated 93% of web3 games launched between 2020 and 2026 — reward emissions
that need infinite player growth to hold their value.

**So we built the Avalanche economy differently, on purpose:**

- **Scrip is not redeemable.** It is minted by us, has no exchange rate, and
  cannot be sold. It buys things inside Valor and nothing else.
- That is a security property, not a limitation. GoodDollar — our
  proof-of-unique-human on Celo — does not exist on Avalanche. If Scrip could be
  sold, one person farming fifty wallets would be worth doing on day one.
  Because it cannot, it is not, and we need no identity system to be safe.
- **Prices are denominated in time, not money.** One cleared operation is 100
  Scrip. Every item is a multiple of that, so a booster costs a quarter of a
  mission and the top-tier weapon costs eighty.
- **Any future cash-out will be funded from revenue**, never from minted supply,
  and will sit behind an identity gate. There is deliberately no redemption
  function in the token contract today.

We would rather show Avalanche a working economy we can defend than a token
chart.

## 5. What the grant funds

The reason to be on Avalanche rather than only Celo is that its players show up
for **competition and ownership**, not redistribution. So the Avalanche build is
not our earn loop in a different colour. It is the mode we designed for this
ecosystem and have not yet built.

### Milestone 1 — Staked duels
Two players stake Scrip, the winner takes the pot, the house takes a cut. Value
moves between players rather than out of a pool, which means it creates no sybil
surface and costs us nothing to run. It is also the first sink in Valor that
keeps working after a player owns everything in the shop.

Specced in full, not started. **This is the single highest-value thing the grant
would buy.**

### Milestone 2 — Mobile controls
Most of our players are on phones. Our touch controls are the largest drag on
retention we can measure. Redesigned movement and aiming, shipped and measured.

### Milestone 3 — Tournaments and seasons
Scheduled competitive events with prize pools, leaderboards and on-chain results.
The natural companion to duels, and the format Avalanche's gaming community
already engages with.

### Milestone 4 — Revenue-funded exit
A Scrip-to-AVAX swap in the Bank, funded exclusively by marketplace and duel
revenue, behind an identity gate. This is the step that makes Avalanche earning
real, and we will not ship it before the revenue exists to back it.

UNION ALL SELECT 'avalanche_records', count(*)::text
  FROM battle_chain_records WHERE chain_id = 43114;
